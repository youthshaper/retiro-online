Welcome to Retiro

Plan your retirement as early as you start earning. This is truly a wise advice because in retirement planning, time is the most important resource a person can have. Majority of Filipinos are underprepared for retirement, according to a recent readiness study. The findings suggest that Filipinos should start planning their retirement early and more intentionally, because traditional pensions and savings may fall short against future living and medical expenses.

However, for many young Filipino professionals, retirement planning can seem vague and overwhelming. That’s where Retiro comes in as a comprehensive financial planning tool. It allows users to visually explore how retirement age, savings, investment growth, pension, expenses, and financial legacy interact. Importantly, it is not a prediction tool or a personal investment advisor, but a practical decision-making simulator designed to help you plan and prepare for your future.

Retiro Online is a compact tool for planning and learning. For this prototype, your personal information is processed entirely within your local browser session and is not sent to any external servers.

4. Your Financial Readiness Profile

Retiro does not reduce retirement readiness to a single score. Instead, it first asks the most important question: Can the projected retirement resources sustain the retirement years you have planned? Other indicators help explain the result rather than being added together into one number.

How to read your profile

What the other indicators tell you

Readiness is presented as a profile rather than a composite score because some indicators describe closely related aspects of the same retirement funding outcome. Corpus adequacy is the primary test. Longevity becomes a diagnostic when funding falls short. When the full planned retirement period is funded, additional resources are better understood as a funding margin or surplus, not as “more longevity.” Pension and legacy remain useful contextual indicators.

Year-by-year retirement journey

How to interpret this exploration: Financial feasibility asks whether this retirement age and contribution level satisfy the pathway's financial conditions. Search scope only tells you whether that age is inside Retiro's automatic 60–70 shortlist. An option can therefore be financially feasible while being outside the search range.

Insights: Generally, earlier retirement requires higher annual contribution, and therefore, greater financial preparation. Conversely, delaying retirement can provide more time for accumulation and may reduce the number of years your assets need to support you. The Retiro Optimizer helps make these relationships more understandable and visible.
