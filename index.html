Welcome to Retiro

Plan your retirement as early as you start earning. This is truly a wise advice because in retirement planning, time is the most important resource a person can have. Majority of Filipinos are underprepared for retirement, according to a recent readiness study. This reality calls for a more intentional and proactive preparation, particularly among young Filipino earners, because traditional savings and pensions may not be enough to cover rising living and healthcare costs.

However, for many young Filipinos, retirement planning can seem vague and overwhelming. That’s where Retiro comes in as a comprehensive financial planning tool. It allows users to visually explore how retirement age, savings, investment growth, pension, expenses, and financial legacy interact. Importantly, it is not a prediction tool or a personal investment advisor, but a practical decision-making simulator designed to help you plan and prepare for your future.

Retiro Online is a lightweight tool for financial planning and learning, and can be used in a more granular retirement preparation. For this prototype, your personal information is processed entirely within your local browser session and is not sent to any external servers.

Year-by-year retirement journey

Insights: Generally, earlier retirement requires higher annual contribution, and therefore, greater financial preparation. Conversely, delaying retirement can provide more time for accumulation and may reduce the number of years your assets need to support you. The Retiro Optimizer helps make these relationships more understandable and visible.
